goran3f: And here's the afternoon, compressed into one line. Roomler exec, the same command, every machine.

goran3f: There are four gates: your organization's switch, your own permission, the device's policy, and a switch on the machine itself that only whoever holds it can flip.

# [The loop again, with a real fix: a service restart on all of them.]
