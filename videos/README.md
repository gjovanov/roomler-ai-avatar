# Videos made with it

The sources of real videos made with this toolkit, kept as worked examples: the script (`script.txt`,
the form `tools/voice/lines.ts` reads) and the brief (`brief.yaml`, every claim the video makes with its
source). The screen recordings, voices and renders are not here: media never enters the repository, and
each folder's `out/` is ignored.

| Folder | What it is | Presenter | Made with |
|---|---|---|---|
| [wayland-asahi](wayland-asahi/) | Roomler controlling a Wayland desktop on a Mac mini (Asahi Linux) from a browser tab, at 56 fps in software ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/q1Fgc3nM3W4)) | Goran, as two AI avatars with his consent (`goran-r5-close`, `goran-mint-jacket-talk`, in a registry of his own: `AVATAR_PRESENTERS`), in his own cloned voice | the [demo-first-scriptwriter](../.claude/skills/demo-first-scriptwriter/SKILL.md) skill's draft, rewritten by him; `talk.ts --size 512x768 --group-gap 1.1 --audio-scale 0.9`; `compose16.ts --layout tutor` |
| [avatar-making-of](avatar-making-of/) | How these videos are made: the script, the voice, the face, the real screen recordings and the compose, with the mistakes that taught each rule ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/eTX3KQ3Ogys)) | Goran, as the same two AI avatars, in his own cloned voice | a script written for him in the style of his own, plus his line; the same `talk.ts` and `compose16.ts` settings |
| [macbook-120fps](macbook-120fps/) | 120 fps on a MacBook, from a browser tab: the Mac's media engine encodes in about 6 ms ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/dSLNEoQWLtA)) | Goran, as the same two AI avatars, in his own cloned voice | a script drafted for him and rewritten by him, his own recording for the "work on it" block; the same `talk.ts` and `compose16.ts` settings |
| [mesh-network](mesh-network/) | The private network (a Tailscale alternative): peers, direct vs relay, SSH and remote commands from Windows to a MacBook, a note written over remote desktop and downloaded two ways ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/XFN_-7ZvUHA)) | Goran, as two AI avatars in a white polo ([`goran-home-polo`](../presenters/goran-home-polo.png), [`goran-library-polo`](../presenters/goran-library-polo.png)), in his own cloned voice at his chosen pace (`goran3f`) | real terminal output replayed as a typed terminal (`term.ts`), real screen recordings of the MacBook in a browser tab; the same `talk.ts` and `compose16.ts` settings |
| [windows-120fps](windows-120fps/) | Windows remote desktop past 60 fps: Windows capture hands an app a frame at most every 16 ms unless asked, Roomler now asks (59 to 118 fps capture), the GPU clocking down sent a trial back to 60, and the next release pinned the clocks and passed at 112 fps ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/ihCCwcBv-nE)) | Goran, as two AI avatars in a red polo (`goran-r5-redpolo`, `goran-mint-redpolo`, kept in his private registry), in his own cloned voice (`goran3f`) | a script drafted for him overnight and approved by his instruction; real terminal takes and the agent's own session logs; the same `talk.ts` and `compose16.ts` settings |
| [podcast-studio](podcast-studio/) | A podcast in a studio: an invented host, Helen, interviews Goran about Roomler, from the TeamViewer story to 120 fps on a MacBook and on Windows, 56 fps in software on Linux, the private network, who it is for and how to install it, with the earlier videos' real recordings full frame ([on YouTube](https://youtu.be/9ai4TUcspM0)) | Helen, an invented AI host (a generated face, a synthetic voice), and Goran as his red-polo AI avatar with his consent, in his own cloned voice (`goran3f`) | the studio conversation format ([the skill's reference](../.claude/skills/avatar-video/references/podcast.md)): two cameras, listening clips driven by the model's trained silence, clips chained with `talk.ts --motion` so no cut jumps; five editions, the last two re-voicing a few lines and reusing 46 of 72 clips |

A voice that belongs to a real person is named in a script (`goran3`, `goran3f`) but never stored here: the
renderer finds it in a private folder (`AVATAR_VOICES_DIR`, [docs/real-person.md](../docs/real-person.md)),
so these scripts render only on a machine that holds that voice with its owner's consent.
